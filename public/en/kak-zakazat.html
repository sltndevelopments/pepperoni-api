How to order halal products wholesale

Kazan Delicacies LLC supplies 65 SKUs of halal meat products and bakery from the Kazan plant. The steps below repeat terms already published on the site and in the facts registry. Transit times in days and prepayment percentages are omitted: they are not in the verified data.

Steps

  1. Choose items in the catalog or the price list. Prices match the product cards. Files: Excel, PDF.
  2. Send an enquiry by phone +7 987 217-02-02, e-mail info@kazandelikates.tatar, WhatsApp or the form on a category page. State city, channel and approximate volume.
  3. Sales confirms availability, pallet mix and the live catalog price. The order price is fixed in the invoice.
  4. Basis is EXW Kazan. The buyer pays freight; lead time is agreed for the specific order.
  5. Shipping set: halal certificate, declaration of conformity, veterinary certificate (Mercury), Chestny ZNAK marking, electronic waybill (ETrN).

Minimum order

Wholesale: one pallet; 1 pallet. Volumes are quoted in pallet multiples.

Private label: from 5 т. See the private-label page.

Delivery and payment

Basis is EXW Kazan. The buyer collects from the Kazan warehouse or arranges freight at their own cost. Lead time is named per order.

Payment terms are fixed in the invoice. The catalog lists reference export prices in USD, KZT, UZS, KGS, BYN, AZN.

More on the basis and export currencies: delivery.

Documents

  • DUM RT halal certificate
  • Declaration of conformity
  • Veterinary certificate in Mercury (VSD)
  • Chestny ZNAK marking
  • Electronic waybill (ETrN)

Certificate list: /en/halal.

Samples and pilot shipment

Samples before a contract are agreed individually — assortment, volume and how the sample is sent. The catalog has no separate “sample price”.

A pilot shipment is a normal wholesale order: one pallet; 1 pallet. Same EXW Kazan basis, same shipping documents.

State city, channel and SKUs from the price list. Retail chains: /en/dlya-setey. Distributors: /en/dlya-distributorov. HoReCa: /en/dlya-horeca.
