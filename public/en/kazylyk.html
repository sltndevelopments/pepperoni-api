FAQ

What meat is kazylyk made from?

Horse meat and horse fat, plus the curing mix and spices listed above. The exact wording is on the product page and the label.

Why do the two SKUs have different shelf lives?

The catalog records 180 days for the 200 g gift box and 30 days for the 100 g sliced pack, both at 0–12°C. After opening follow the label.

What is the minimum order?

One pallet; a mixed pallet with other chilled SKUs is possible. Prices are EXW Kazan. Delivery, samples and payment terms are agreed per order.
