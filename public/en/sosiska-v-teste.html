FAQ

Which SKUs are on this page?
Only KD-018 (casing-free sausage for dough) and KD-061 (ready sausage in dough). KD-017 is a link — breakfast sausage with casing.
Is there a post-bake slice photo?
No. When a real oven-test photo exists it will appear here. Weight, shelf life and price are on the SKU cards.
How is halal confirmed?
Halal DUM RT No. 0008 (meat, poultry, sausages, pepperoni, semi-finished; 2 Oct 2026–1 Oct 2029), No. 614A/2024 (meat; 17 Feb 2024–16 Feb 2027), No. 884A/2025 (bakery and ready meals; 27 Dec 2025–26 Dec 2026), additional registry numbers 976A/2026 and 1002/2026 (product scope is not published as text), HACCP, ISO 22000:2018 (FSMS-2351/A), Made in Russia No. SS.003181 (until 23 Oct 2028), TR CU 021/2011. Bakery SKU uses No. 884A/2025; the meat sausage uses No. 0008 and No. 614A/2024.
