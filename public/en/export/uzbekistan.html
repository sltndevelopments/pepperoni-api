🇺🇿 Halal meat from Russia to Uzbekistan

Facility readiness · Cerberus · not a current shipment

Uzbekistan is listed among destinations for which facility No. RU-016/SB88273 is attested in the Rosselkhoznadzor Cerberus registry. That is a facility attestation, not a promise of a consignment and not a current shipment. Current shipments are Kazakhstan, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan.

Certification and market access

Halal DUM RT No. 0008 (meat, poultry, sausages, pepperoni, semi-finished; 2 Oct 2026–1 Oct 2029), No. 614A/2024 (meat; 17 Feb 2024–16 Feb 2027), No. 884A/2025 (bakery and ready meals; 27 Dec 2025–26 Dec 2026), HACCP, ISO 22000:2018 (FSMS-2351/A), Made in Russia No. SS.003181 (until 23 Oct 2028), TR CU 021/2011

Certificate recognition in Uzbekistan is confirmed per shipment with the importer. This page does not claim destination-authority approval.

The facility is in the Cerberus registry (No. RU-016/SB88273) and attested for export to Uzbekistan. Attestation is not a shipping timetable.

Uzbekistan is not an EAEU member; customs are outside the union and confirmed per consignment. No day-count is published here.

Logistics facts we can state

Default basis is EXW Kazan (Incoterms 2020). The buyer arranges freight, or we agree otherwise. We do not publish transit days or a regular Tashkent schedule: the evidence registry has no confirmed transit time and no volume promise.

No som (UZS) offer on this page. Base price is RUB EXW; invoice currency is by contract. Catalog reference currencies are not a UZS offer.
