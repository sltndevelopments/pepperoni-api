2. Terms

The terms “personal data”, “operator”, “processing”, “personal data subject”, “consent” and “destruction” are used as defined in Article 3 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

3. Data subjects and PD categories

3.1. Website visitors and form submitters

  • name (if provided);
  • phone number (required);
  • message text (product interest, volume, city, or other details you enter);
  • page URL from which the form was sent;
  • optional experiment/landing technical ID;
  • fact and time of consent;
  • submitter IP address (anti-abuse / rate limiting).

The form field “company” is an anti-spam honeypot and is not intended for human input. Email, Telegram handle or company name are processed only if you type them into the message or phone field yourself.

3.2. Technical data of visitors

  • IP address, User-Agent, referrer;
  • pages visited, click and conversion events;
  • cookies and similar identifiers set by analytics/ads tools (see Section 8).

3.3. Public API consumers

The API at api.pepperoni.tatar is public and does not require registration. Server access logs may store IP address, User-Agent, request URL, response code and timestamp for security and abuse prevention. No API user account is created. The product catalog does not contain customer PD.

3.4. Special categories

The Operator does not intentionally collect special categories of PD (health, ethnicity, political views, etc.) or biometric PD. Please do not submit such information via website forms.

12. Your rights

Under Arts. 14–15 and 20–21 of 152-FZ you may request information about processing, correction, blocking or destruction of PD, withdraw consent, and complain to Roskomnadzor or a court.

Email info@kazandelikates.tatar with subject “Personal Data Request”, your name (or the name used in the lead), a reply contact and your request. The Operator responds within the timelines of Art. 20 of 152-FZ (as a rule, within 10 business days, extendable as permitted by law).

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal and does not stop processing that must continue on other legal bases (contract, law).
