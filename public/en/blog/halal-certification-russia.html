On customer pages we name 614A for meat and 884A for bakery. 976A and 1002 are extra active registry numbers — we do not invent a category for them.

Plant controls besides Halal: HACCP, ISO 22000:2018, TR CU 021/2011. There is no kosher certificate.

What we send with a lot

Owner-approved pack: Halal certificate, declaration of conformity, veterinary papers in «Меркурий», «Честный знак» marking, electronic waybill (ЭТрН). Ask sales for the copies that match the SKU you order.

A certificate number without the registry card is not enough. Open the card, match the legal name ООО «Казанские Деликатесы» and the dates.

What this page does not claim

  • foreign Halal marks besides DUM RT — we do not hold them;
  • that ЦДУМ / SMR “always accept” a DUM RT card — we do not publish other bodies’ rules;
  • fees, months-to-certificate or a generic slaughter SOP — not in our public file;
  • export to the Middle East as a current lane. CIS directions (Kazakhstan, Belarus, Uzbekistan) are the stated markets. Preparing to supply; Rosselkhoznadzor permits are pending.
