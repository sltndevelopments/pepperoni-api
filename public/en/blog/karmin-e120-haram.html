Carmine (E120) is haram, not halal

26 August 2026 · Kazan Delicacies

Carmine is not halal. It is haram. The Halal Committee of DUM RT classifies colour E120 (cochineal) as forbidden in food. Kazan Delicacies does not use it.

In one line: carmine = haram, not halal. Current SKU ingredients on pepperoni.tatar contain no carmine, E120, or cochineal.

Is carmine halal?

No. Carmine is haram. The DUM RT Halal Committee says E120 is forbidden for Muslims and belongs to the category of haram. “Natural” does not make it lawful.

Carmine is a red food colour listed as carmine, carmines, E120, or cochineal. It comes from carminic acid of female cochineal insects.

A red slice of sausage does not prove E120. Colour often comes from meat, paprika, smoking, or a colour fixative (nitrite salt). Read the ingredient list.

Position of the DUM RT Halal Committee

Official text: “Dangerous red, or why carmine is forbidden in Islam” on halalrt.ru (press service; reviewed by Ilgiz Bagautdinov and Bulat Mubarakov).

“Carmine is registered as food additive E120 and is forbidden for Muslims to consume.”

“It follows that carmine belongs to the category of haram.”

Same rule in composition requirements, item 5. Ibn Abidin allows cochineal for textiles, not for food.

Why we do not use it

We make halal products in Kazan, certificate No. 614A/2024 from the same Committee. A haram colour does not go on the line. That is the whole catalog — not a special “carmine-free” range.

How to check

  1. Product card on pepperoni.tatar — Ingredients.
  2. On the pack: carmine, carmines, E120, cochineal.
  3. Do not confuse with paprika or sodium nitrite.
