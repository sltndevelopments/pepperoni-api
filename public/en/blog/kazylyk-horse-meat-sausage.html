Kazylyk — what it is and where to buy wholesale from the maker

What kazylyk is

Kazylyk is dry-cured horse sausage. In Tatar cooking it is a ready-to-slice cold product: served on a meat plate, in a festive assortment, or as a gift pack. It is not cooked-smoked sausage and not a grill frankfurter.

In this catalogue kazylyk is a chilled dry-cured product in gift packaging. Frozen horse grill sausages KD-008 (80 g × 6) are a different SKU, process and shelf life.

If the query is “what is kazylyk” — dry-cured horse sausage. If the query is “buy kazylyk wholesale” — the two SKUs below and sales.

How it differs from our pepperoni

Pepperoni here is cooked-smoked chicken: KD-013 sliced 0.5 kg and KD-014 stick 0.8 kg, stored at −18 °C. Kazylyk is dry-cured horse meat and horse fat, chilled at 0–12 °C. Different line, composition, shelf life and kitchen use.

KD-044 keeps 180 days; KD-045 keeps 30 days. Those numbers are on the SKU cards. We do not publish a separate “after opening” clock.

HoReCa: kazylyk is cold service or a gift stick. Pizza ovens are the pepperoni line.

How to buy
