What Is Halal Pepperoni: Meat, Product Types and How It Differs from Regular Pepperoni

March 27, 2026 · updated September 11, 2026

Halal pepperoni is pepperoni without pork, made from meat or poultry slaughtered under Islamic rules at a plant with a halal certificate. In the kitchen it is the same topping for pizza, sandwiches and rolls as regular pepperoni; what differs is the raw material and the control over where every ingredient comes from. This article explains what "halal pepperoni" means as a category and what the Kazan Delicacies product is actually made of.

Key facts

  • Meat: any halal meat except pork — beef, lamb, poultry; Kazan Delicacies uses chicken
  • Product type: dry-cured (US classic) or cooked-smoked; ours is cooked-smoked
  • Formats: 0.5 kg sliced (KD-013) and 0.8 kg whole stick (KD-014), 50–55 mm diameter
  • Storage: –18 °C, 360 days
  • Certification: Halal DUM RT No. 614A/2024, HACCP, ISO 22000:2018

What "halal pepperoni" means

"Pepperoni" describes a type of sausage: finely minced meat, salt, paprika and hot spices, thinly sliced onto pizza. "Halal" describes the origin of the raw material and the way it is produced: no pork or pork fat, meat from animals and poultry slaughtered under Islamic rules, no alcohol in the recipe, and control over every ingredient from the meat to the casing and spices. Compliance is confirmed by a certifying body that audits the plant.

The standard does not prescribe which meat halal pepperoni must be made from. Producers use beef, lamb, poultry or blends. When sourcing, read the ingredient list of the specific product rather than treating the word "halal" as a description of the recipe.

Dry-cured vs cooked-smoked: what changes in the kitchen

Classic American pepperoni is dry-cured: the mince is fermented and dried for several weeks, giving a dense, tangy product. Cooked-smoked pepperoni is heat-treated and smoked, so it is ready to eat straight from production, softer on the cut and needs no ripening. For a pizzeria the practical differences are in how the slice behaves on the pizza (density, fat release) and in logistics: our product is stored frozen. Exact slicing parameters and oven behaviour are recorded in a separate test — see pepperoni slicing specs.

How halal pepperoni differs from regular pepperoni

Regular pepperoni is traditionally made from pork and beef. Halal pepperoni contains no pork, and the meat comes from animals and poultry slaughtered under Islamic rules. Differences in taste and colour follow from the chosen raw material, not from the word "halal" itself. Technologically the difference lies in controlling the origin of every ingredient and in certifying the plant — not in slicing or use: halal pepperoni goes on pizza exactly like regular pepperoni.

What to check when sourcing

Certificate: ask for a valid halal certificate and confirm it is issued to the manufacturer and covers the product you buy. Ours is DUM RT No. 614A/2024.

Ingredients and product type: which meat, dry-cured or cooked-smoked, casing or not. This determines taste, oven behaviour and storage.

Format and storage: sliced or stick, pack weight, temperature and shelf life. Ask how the product behaves after thawing and opening.

Samples: test the product in your own oven before signing a contract — the only check that replaces any description.
