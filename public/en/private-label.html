How a private label is launched — 5 steps

  1. Brief & specificationYou describe the product: category, format, weight, composition, retailer requirements, packaging. We sign an NDA.
  2. Samples & tastingWe produce samples to the brief; your technologist and buyer approve the final recipe. Number of iterations and timing — per contract.
  3. Label & documentsYour designer prepares artwork to our format. We issue the TR CU declaration, halal certificate, test reports and Chestny ZNAK codes.
  4. First runFrom 5 tonnes per SKU; shipment EXW Kazan with veterinary certificates (Mercury) and electronic waybill.
  5. Serial supplySchedule and price are fixed in the contract; the recipe is locked in the specification and identical batch to batch.

Frequently asked

Do you only produce halal? Which meats do you work with?

Yes — halal only. The whole plant is certified by the Halal Standards Committee of the Muslim Religious Board of Tatarstan (No. 614A/2024); there is no pork on site. Meat bases in the current catalog: chicken, beef, turkey, horse meat, and lamb in selected SKUs. One product works for Muslim and non-Muslim shoppers alike — no second assortment matrix.

What is the minimum private-label run (MOQ)?

From 5 tonnes per SKU. Production capacity is 12 tonnes per shift. Pilot volume, schedule and price are agreed in the contract.

How long does a launch take and are samples paid?

Stages and timelines are agreed individually in the contract — they depend on the category, recipe and packaging. Test samples are agreed individually and are usually free of charge.

We are a meat processor. Can we supply our own raw material?

Yes, we also work on a toll-manufacturing basis: you deliver raw material with veterinary certificates, we produce, pack and return the finished product with our documents.

Which documents does the customer receive?

All of them: EAEU declaration of conformity (TR CU 021/2011), DUM RT halal certificate, veterinary certificates (Mercury system), Chestny ZNAK marking codes, electronic waybill (ETrN), and laboratory test reports.

Can you produce to our recipe and under our product names?

Yes — that is how the «Aslam» line for OMPK JSC works: traditional sausages to the customer's recipes and names (no pepperoni in that line). Pepperoni under a customer's brand is a separate product, made to our catalog recipe or to your specification.

Do you export private-label products?

Prices are EXW Kazan. Current export directions are the CIS (Kazakhstan, Belarus, Uzbekistan). Supplies to the Gulf states are at the preparation stage: the halal document package is ready and the Rosselkhoznadzor export permit is being obtained.
