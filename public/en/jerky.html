Packing — what the shopper actually sees

The plant already runs vacuum, modified atmosphere (MAP), flowpack, skin and corrugated cases. For jerky that means a retail pack in your design and a transport case in the same shipment.

Flowpack

A pillow bag for sticks and portioned strips. Checkout, petrol, impulse. Film and window follow the artwork.

Doypack

A standing pouch, often with a zipper. The main retail and marketplace format. Fill weight is in the spec.

Vacuum / foodservice

Clear vacuum for kitchens, wholesale and pack-on-site. Useful when a chain fills its own bags or feeds a commissary.

Stick multipack

Several sticks in a shared film or a display box. For the till and the sports channel.

Questions

Will the jerky be halal?

Yes. The plant is certified by the Halal Standard Committee of DUM RT, certificate #614A/2024. There is no pork, no pork fat and no alcohol anywhere in the production. We send a copy of the certificate with the terms.

Which meats do you use?

Beef, chicken, turkey, lamb and horse — all halal. Species, fat level and spices are locked in the specification after the sample.

Is there a ready article in the pepperoni.tatar catalogue?

There is no serial jerky SKU in the open catalogue. This is a contract line: sample and label are built for the customer’s brand. Nearby in the catalogue are kazylyk and other dry-cured / sausage items that show the meat and packing expertise.

How is jerky different from kazylyk or basturma?

Kazylyk is horse sausage (we have gift SKUs). Basturma is a whole-muscle delicacy. Jerky is a snack: thin strips, chips or sticks in a retail pack. The recipes are not interchangeable.

What is the minimum order?

Pilot — from 500 kg per month per item. Serial — 2–5 tonnes per month. Several SKUs (for example beef + chicken) can share a schedule; that is agreed separately.

Which packs can you run?

Vacuum, MAP, flowpack, skin, corrugated case — those are plant capabilities. For jerky, buyers most often ask for flowpack, doypack and vacuum bulk. You set fill weight and film.

What is the shelf life?

It cannot be stated honestly as one number without a spec and a pack type. Shelf life and temperature are locked in the specification for the chosen film and product moisture. This page does not invent “12 months for all jerky”.

Can you pack under our brand?

Yes: white label, private label, OEM to your spec, and joint development. We change meat, geometry, heat, pack and label.

Which documents do you provide?

DUM RT halal, an EAEU declaration to TR CU 021/2011 for the customer’s product, veterinary e-documents, HACCP and ISO 22000:2018 evidence, the specification and batch reports, and marking artwork.
