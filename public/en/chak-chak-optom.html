Chak-chak wholesale — two catalog packs

Two rows from Google Sheets, not from ad copy: KD-053 «Chak-Chak (plastic pack)» and KD-054 «Chak-Chak (craft gift box)». Both sit in the Bakery section, Tatar national pastry category. This is a ready sweet stored at ambient temperature, not a frozen pie.

What differs between the packs

The catalog names record only the formats: plastic pack and kraft gift box. Both rows weigh 300 g. Box count: 10 pcs (KD-053) and 10 pcs (KD-054). Composition and nutrition cells are empty — this page does not invent them.

Shelf life from the catalog shelfLife field: 90 days (KD-053) and 90 days (KD-054). Storage: up to +18°C, humidity 75%. That is not −18 °C and not 360 days, unlike frozen pastry.
