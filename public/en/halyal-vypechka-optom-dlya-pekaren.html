Halal bakery wholesale for bakeries and coffee shops

A buyer article for a bakery or coffee shop: which frozen rows and sausage-in-dough SKUs are in the live catalog, which certificate covers bakery, and where to send the enquiry. Oven temperature and minutes are not published — the evidence registry has no reproducible bake test.

Who this page is for

  • A bakery that needs freezer stock and either a casing-free sausage for its own dough or a ready sausage roll — KD-018/KD-061 details on /en/sosiska-v-teste.
  • A coffee shop with an oven: the freezer-to-counter scenario stays on /en/zamorozhennaya-vypechka-dlya-kofeen.
  • The full Tatar line is the hub /en/vyipechka-halyal. Chak-chak (not frozen) is /en/chak-chak-optom.

Bakery certificate

Halal certification by DUM RT, certificate No. 884A/2025 — confectionery, bakery and ready meals; valid 27 Dec 2025–26 Dec 2026. Verify on /en/halal. KD-018 uses the meat certificates (Halal DUM RT No. 0008 and No. 614A/2024), not the bakery certificate.

FAQ

Which SKUs are in this article?
21 distinct SKUs: chilled KD-018 and 20 frozen bakery rows (KD-061 is in the frozen list, not counted twice). Chak-chak KD-053/054 is on /en/chak-chak-optom — it is not frozen.
Which certificate covers bakery?
Halal certification by DUM RT, certificate No. 884A/2025 — confectionery, bakery and ready meals; valid 27 Dec 2025–26 Dec 2026. Verify on /en/halal. KD-018 is covered by the meat certificates.
Where is sausage in dough on its own?
On /en/sosiska-v-teste: only KD-018 and KD-061, with no invented oven test.
