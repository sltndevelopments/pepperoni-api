Why a Certificate of Conformity matters

In Russia, religious halal attestations coexist with certification against the national standard GOST R 70401-2022 carried out by certification bodies within their accredited scope.

Religious attestation

Local Attestation / Certificate

Issued under a religious or voluntary scheme according to that scheme’s own rules and used to confirm halal status within that system. It does not by itself mean certification against GOST R 70401-2022.

Accredited certification

Certificate of Conformity

Voluntary certification document confirming conformity with the requirements of national standard GOST R 70401-2022. It is issued by an accredited certification body following the conformity-assessment procedures required by the applicable certification scheme.

Rosaccreditation record FSA RA.RU.170001: certification body status

The certification body "Halal Committee of DUM RT" is accredited by the Federal Accreditation Service (Rosaccreditation) with record number RA.RU.170001.

What the FSA accreditation guarantees:

  • State verification of competence. The body has proven the qualifications of its auditors, audit methodologies, and testing independence under GOST R ISO/IEC 17065.
  • Verifiable certification-body status. Record RA.RU.170001 allows customers to verify the body’s accreditation in the Rosaccreditation register. The halal certificate does not replace mandatory declarations, veterinary documents, or other documents required for a specific product or shipment.
  • Practical B2B value. The certificate can form part of a supplier-qualification or quality-audit documentation pack. Each retailer, HoReCa operator, importer, or customer sets its own requirements for halal certification.
  • Annual surveillance audits. The 3-year certificate mandates regular annual inspections of the production site and product sample testing.

Scope of Certification: Categories BI, BIII

Certificate No. 0008 operates under Certification Scheme 1 and covers two core categories of meat processing:

Category BI

Processing of perishable animal products: chilled and frozen meat, poultry, beef cuts, broiler chicken parts, minced meats, and semi-finished products.

Category BIII

Long shelf-life animal products: sausages, dry-cured delicacies, pizza pepperoni, cooked-smoked items, frankfurters, and culinary meat items.

Core principles of halal production

  • Permissible raw materials: only beef, broiler chicken, turkey, and horse meat from approved suppliers.
  • Canonical slaughter: manual slaughter by qualified Muslims invoking the name of Allah ("Bismillah, Allahu Akbar"), adhering to humane handling and full bleeding.
  • Isolated production: the plant processes only halal raw materials. Contact, mixing, or shared storage with non-halal items is strictly excluded.
  • Clean recipes: made without alcohol-containing additives, without carmine dye (E120), without artificial thickeners, and without transglutaminase ("meat glue").
  • Food safety integration: operations certified under ISO 22000:2018 (HACCP) with monitoring of critical control points.

Using the certificate for export shipments

GOST R 70401-2022 is a Russian national standard. Certificate No. 0008 does not by itself guarantee automatic recognition in every country. For each export shipment, the requirements of the destination country, importer, and competent halal authority must be checked separately, including certificate recognition and accompanying documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Halal Certificate of Conformity No. 0008?

It is an official document verifying compliance of Kazan Delicacies meat products with standard GOST R 70401-2022. Issued by the "Halal Committee of DUM RT", valid from 02 Oct 2026 to 01 Oct 2029.

What is the importance of accreditation record FSA RA.RU.170001?

This is the certification body’s registry record in the Federal Accreditation Service (Rosaccreditation) and allows its accreditation status and scope to be verified. It does not mean automatic acceptance of the certificate by every retailer, regulator, importer, or foreign halal authority.

What is included in Annexes 1–9 to the certificate?

Annexes 1–9 specify the complete range: cooked-smoked, semi-smoked and cooked sausages (Annex 1), poultry products (Annex 2), grill sausages and child nutrition (Annex 3), meat semi-finished products, pelmeni and manti (Annex 4), burgers and kebabs (Annex 5), dry-cured kazylyk and pepperoni (Annex 6), beef, lamb and horse meat delicacies (Annex 7), shawarma products and rolls (Annex 8), boneless beef cuts and broiler parts (Annex 9).

How is raw material authenticity tested in the laboratory?

Testing utilizes polymerase chain reaction (PCR) DNA analysis to detect and exclude non-permitted animal species or unapproved biological contaminants.

Is Certificate No. 0008 suitable for retail chains and HoReCa?

The certificate can be included in a supplier-audit documentation pack and used to substantiate halal status. Retail chains, HoReCa operators, franchise networks, and importers set their own qualification requirements, so automatic acceptance is not claimed.
