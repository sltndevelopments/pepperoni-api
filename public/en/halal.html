Why a Certificate of Conformity matters

Halal conformity assessment has made a qualitative step forward: from internal voluntary testimonies to official national standard certification.

Previous format

Local Attestation / Certificate

Issued under voluntary certification systems according to internal religious rules. Provided canonical verification for individuals but lacked unified state recognition.

Current state format

Certificate of Conformity

Official state document confirming rigorous conformity to national standard GOST R 70401-2022. Issued by a state-accredited body on the basis of laboratory testing and on-site facility audits.

Accreditation by Rosaccreditation (FSA RA.RU.170001)

The certification body "Halal Committee of DUM RT" is accredited by the Federal Accreditation Service (Rosaccreditation) with record number RA.RU.170001.

What the FSA accreditation guarantees:

  • State verification of competence. The body has proven the qualifications of its auditors, audit methodologies, and testing independence under GOST R ISO/IEC 17065.
  • Legal authority for government oversight. The certificate holds full legal authority with Rosselkhoznadzor, Rospotrebnadzor, the Mercury traceability system, and customs agencies.
  • Acceptance by retail chains and HoReCa. Large supermarket chains and restaurant networks require certificates issued by accredited certification bodies listed in the Rosaccreditation register.
  • Annual surveillance audits. The 3-year certificate mandates regular annual inspections of the production site and product sample testing.

Scope of Certification: Categories BI, BIII and Annexes 1–9

Certificate No. 0008 operates under Certification Scheme 1 and covers two core categories of meat processing:

Category BI

Processing of perishable animal products: chilled and frozen meat, poultry, beef cuts, broiler chicken parts, minced meats, and semi-finished products.

Category BIII

Long shelf-life animal products: sausages, dry-cured delicacies, pizza pepperoni, cooked-smoked items, frankfurters, and culinary meat items.

According to Annexes 1–9 of the certificate, the complete production range is certified:

  • Sausage products: beef and poultry pepperoni, cooked-smoked sausages, dry-cured kazylyk, cervelats, and sausages.
  • Poultry products: smoked and roasted delicacies, broiler chicken cuts.
  • Semi-finished meat products: burger patties, minced meat, meatballs, flash-frozen molded products.
  • Culinary meat products: prepared dishes and fillings.
  • Dough-enclosed semi-finished products: pelmeni, manti, and traditional Tatar pastries.
  • Beef cuts and broiler parts: raw cuts of guaranteed halal integrity.

Core principles of halal production

  • Permissible raw materials: only beef, broiler chicken, turkey, and horse meat from approved suppliers.
  • Canonical slaughter: manual slaughter by qualified Muslims invoking the name of Allah ("Bismillah, Allahu Akbar"), adhering to humane handling and full bleeding.
  • Isolated production: the plant processes only halal raw materials. Contact, mixing, or shared storage with non-halal items is strictly excluded.
  • Clean recipes: made without alcohol-containing additives, without carmine dye (E120), without artificial thickeners, and without transglutaminase ("meat glue").
  • Food safety integration: operations certified under ISO 22000:2018 (HACCP) with monitoring of critical control points.

International recognition & SMIIC standards

Standard GOST R 70401-2022 was harmonized with international standard OIC/SMIIC 1 (Standards and Metrology Institute for Islamic Countries). The Halal Committee of DUM RT maintains foreign partnerships, ensuring recognition across international Muslim markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Halal Certificate of Conformity No. 0008?

It is an official document verifying compliance of Kazan Delicacies meat products with standard GOST R 70401-2022. Issued by the "Halal Committee of DUM RT", valid from 02 Oct 2026 to 01 Oct 2029.

What is the importance of accreditation record FSA RA.RU.170001?

This is the unique registry record of the certification body in the Federal Accreditation Service (Rosaccreditation), proving government audit of the body under GOST R ISO/IEC 17065 and ensuring acceptance by federal retailers and oversight bodies.

How is raw material authenticity tested in the laboratory?

Testing utilizes polymerase chain reaction (PCR) DNA analysis to detect and exclude non-permitted animal species or unapproved biological contaminants.

Is Certificate No. 0008 suitable for retail chains and HoReCa?

Yes. The certificate fulfills supplier qualification requirements for federal retail chains, hospitality operators, and pizza franchise networks.
