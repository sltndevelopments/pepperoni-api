# Wholesale inquiry

This is B2B EXW Kazan. There is no cart, no x402 paywall, and no agent checkout. Collect facts, then submit.

## Required facts

- Company name and country
- SKU or product family (pepperoni / private-label jerky / sausages / patties / bakery)
- Volume (kg or cases) and Incoterms if they have a preference (default EXW Kazan)
- Contact: email or WhatsApp/phone

## How to send

1. MCP tool `submit_inquiry` on `https://api.pepperoni.tatar/api/mcp`
2. Or tell the human to write info@kazandelikates.tatar / WhatsApp +7 987 217-02-02 (`https://wa.me/79872170202`)

Do not promise delivery into the UAE/KSA as a registered local seller. Do not invent prices if the live catalog call failed — say the sheet is the source of truth.
