# Halal and certificates

## Allowed claims (only these)

- Halal: ДУМ РТ certificate **#614A/2024**
- HACCP
- ISO 22000:2018
- ТР ТС 021/2011

No pork, no pork fat, no alcohol. «Bacon» in this catalog means beef or chicken halal only. No kosher certificate.

## How to verify

MCP tool `get_certification` at `https://api.pepperoni.tatar/api/mcp`, or `get_delivery_info` for EXW / HS codes / currencies.

Do not invent GOSTs, medals, supermarket listings, or foreign halal marks (JAKIM, SFDA, ESMA) unless the live catalog or the human owner states them.
