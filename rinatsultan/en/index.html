01 / work

What I do

Where a plant loses time and attention. Which decisions repeat every day. What a system can take — and what it must not, because a mistake is expensive. The outcome is a plan, not a pile of toys.

Analytics with AI

The data already lives in the ERP, spreadsheets and people’s heads. I connect it so a director sees the day, instead of waiting for a month-end report.

Faster sales with agents

An agent does the first mile: finds companies, gathers facts, writes the first email, prepares a brief. A manager steps in when the talk and the trust are human.

05 / formats

Three ways to start

01

Diagnosis

For a leader who sees AI changing the industry and does not want to buy another tool blind.

In 2–3 weeks I map processes, data, tools already bought, and vendor proposals.

You leave with a map of opportunities and a sequence.

02

90-day pilot

One process you can measure: sales, analytics, inbound, the website, shop-floor control.

We freeze the baseline first. In 90 days the change shows in numbers, not slides.

A working loop and clear economics.

03

Change architecture

When you need a new operating model, not one isolated case.

I sit on the owner’s side: sequence, success criteria, oversight of internal and external teams. I do not replace integrators — I keep them building the system the business needs, not the largest quote for themselves.

No more than 2–3 companies at once.

06 / who I work with

Who I work with

Mid-size and large manufacturers and FMCG companies — sales, data, management and export inside them.

Usually hundreds of millions to a few billion RUB in revenue: already too complex to run “on people”, still entrepreneurial enough for the owner to change how the company is built.

  • growth keeps demanding more people;
  • data exists, decisions are still made by feel;
  • AI was tried — the result was a pile of disconnected tools;
  • automation or a new market is next.

Who I am not for

  • Anyone who wants to “implement a neural net” because the strategy deck says so.
  • Anyone waiting for a presentation instead of a working loop.
  • Anyone who will not change processes and only wants to buy a tool.
  • Micro-business — no budget, no team. Exception: a project I personally want to study.

If you are in the left column — write. If you are in the right one, it is kinder not to start.
